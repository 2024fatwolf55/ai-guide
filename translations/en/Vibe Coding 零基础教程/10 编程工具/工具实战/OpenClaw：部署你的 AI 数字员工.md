## How to Install OpenClaw

A lot of people think that if you want to use OpenClaw, you need a physical computer running 24/7. But actually that’s not necessary at all. **A single cloud server is enough**, and it’s even more stable—no worrying about power outages or losing the network, and you can command it from your phone anytime.

On February 8, Baidu Intelligent Cloud launched an **OpenClaw ultra-fast easy deployment solution**. Even if you have zero programming background, you can click a few times and have your own AI digital employee within minutes. It also supports one-click switching between mainstream AI models, and can even connect OpenClaw to QQ, Feishu, DingTalk, and WeCom. **You can direct AI to work just by sending a message from your phone.**

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209170321021.png)

Next I’ll walk everyone through it in practice. Definitely worth bookmarking ⭐️.



## Step-by-Step OpenClaw Deployment

**1) Get a cloud server**

You can think of a cloud server as **a computer sitting in a server room**—always on, always connected, and available for remote access anytime, anywhere.

Let me first share the simplest way to get a server. Open the special “minimal deployment page” Baidu Intelligent Cloud made for OpenClaw. For just **0.01 RMB**, you can grab a **2-core / 4GB / 4Mbps** lightweight application server and try it free for one month.

> Link: https://cloud.baidu.com/product/BCC/moltbot.html

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209143722677.png)

This promotion is limited every day and both new and old users can participate. I guess I got lucky—I managed to grab the deal haha:

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209143544557.png)

After a short wait, the server finishes initializing.

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209143600373.png)

Click the “One-Click Deploy OpenClaw” button and it will take you to the server management page.

If you miss the promotion, don’t be discouraged. You can go into the lightweight application server console and create a server manually.

> Link: https://console.bce.baidu.com/ls/#/ls/instance/create

Important: for the image, make sure you choose the **OpenClaw** application image, and for the server plan, **2 cores and 4GB** is more than enough.

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209144440798.png)

After checkout, the server is created automatically, and just like above, you can enter the server management page.

By this point, you’ve basically obtained an AI employee that’s “about to start work.”



**2) Enable the related services with one click**

On the server management page, click the **Application Management** tab.

The page will tell you that several services need to be enabled, such as Qianfan large models and Cloud Assistant. No need to hunt them down one by one—just click **One-Click Enable**, agree to the terms, and you’re done.

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209144927875.png)



**3) Open the firewall port**

If you want to access the deployed OpenClaw web console, you need to open port `18789` in the server firewall.

Just click the **One-Click Allow** button:

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209145239379.png)



**4) Choose an AI model**

Next, let’s give your AI employee a smart brain~

You can directly choose the model you want from the dropdown. Most mainstream Chinese large models are supported (including DeepSeek). After selecting one, click **Apply**.

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209145623840.png)

The system automatically creates the API key for calling the model and configures everything for you.

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209145857028.png)

Once execution finishes successfully, your OpenClaw is ready to use.

At this point, congratulations—your AI digital employee has officially joined the team!

The whole process only takes a few minutes, and it’s extremely foolproof. The platform clearly put a lot of effort into making it beginner-friendly, as if it was afraid that even one extra step might scare users away.



**5) Chat with your AI employee**

Click **Get Website Address** at the bottom of the page:

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209150158108.png)

Then open the link to enter the OpenClaw web interface:

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209150316824.png)

Now you can chat directly with OpenClaw on the web. For example, you can give it a name first. I called mine “Yupi’s Heavenly Dog”:

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209150631021.png)

As you can see, the AI automatically updated its own identity and will retain that memory. After that, you can keep training it through conversation until it becomes your most capable assistant.

## Remotely Direct AI from Mobile QQ

If you installed OpenClaw through Baidu Intelligent Cloud, connecting it to QQ is very simple and only takes a few steps.

**1) Create a QQ bot**

Open the [QQ Open Platform](https://q.qq.com), register, and log in:

> Link: https://q.qq.com

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209151938145.png)

After logging in successfully, click the “Bot” tab and create a new bot:

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209152043823.png)

Give your bot a nickname and a cute avatar so you can find it easily in QQ later:

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209152207016.png)



**2) Configure the bot**

After creation, enter the bot’s development management page:

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209152253262.png)

Find the **AppID** and **AppSecret**, copy them, and save them somewhere. You’ll need them in a moment.

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209152347726.png)

You also need to add your cloud server’s **public IP** to the IP whitelist, then save it.

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209152537553.png)

You can see the server’s public IP on the Baidu Intelligent Cloud server management page. Be careful not to expose it to others!

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209152435791.png)



**3) Fill in the messaging platform configuration**

On the Baidu Intelligent Cloud server management page, find **Messaging Platform Configuration**, choose **QQ** from the dropdown, fill in the AppID and AppSecret, click **Apply**, and wait for execution to finish.

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209152729389.png)



**4) Grant permission to access the bot**

Go back to the QQ Open Platform and add your QQ account (or QQ group) in the sandbox configuration so it has permission to access the bot:

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209152928094.png)

Then just use QQ to scan the QR code and add the bot:

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209153102382.png)

Now you can directly chat with your AI digital employee on QQ:

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209153537993.png)

After this, all you have to do is lie in bed, open QQ, and direct the AI running on your remote server to work for you. Pretty comfortable, huh~

Besides QQ, OpenClaw also supports Feishu, DingTalk, and WeCom. The setup process is similar. If you need those, check the official tutorials:

## Install Skill Packs for Your AI Employee

The full name of Skills is Agent Skills, and they’ve also become extremely popular in the AI world.

Put simply, Skills are skill packs for AI. They contain carefully designed prompts, code scripts, and all kinds of resource files, allowing AI to perform more professionally on specific tasks. For example, if you equip AI with a PPT-making Skill, it will know how to make presentations.

You can strengthen OpenClaw’s abilities by installing skill packs for it.

**So how do you get and install Skills?**

If you use Baidu Intelligent Cloud to install OpenClaw, installing Skills is very easy.

Baidu Qianfan recently packaged its own AI capabilities as Skills and listed them in OpenClaw’s Skill marketplace, [ClawHub](https://clawhub.ai/skills). At the moment there are 6 official Skills, including Baidu Search, Baidu Encyclopedia, academic retrieval, AI picture-book generation, smart PPT generation, and the Qianfan deep research Agent.

Just go to the Skills configuration on the server management page, and you can search for and install Skills in a very beginner-friendly way:

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209154028274.png)

For example, I installed the Baidu Search and Baidu Encyclopedia Skills. Both are Baidu’s strengths and work well for searching Chinese information sources.

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209154152546.png)

After adding the Skills, go into the Skills settings on the OpenClaw management page and you’ll see that the installation succeeded:

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209160408078.png)

Then I asked “Yupi’s Heavenly Dog” to search for “programmer Yupi”:

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/8a535406f294c23379ee79b0a703b645.jpg)

This time the result was much more reliable. In OpenClaw’s web chat box, you can see that AI called the Baidu Search Skill:

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209160023370.png)

Besides the installation method above, you can also log into the server and manually install Skills with one command:

```bash
npx clawhub@latest install [skill名称]
```

## What Can You Do with OpenClaw?

I suspect many people will finish setting up OpenClaw and then just leave it sitting there, or simply not know what it can actually do. So here I’ll share a few practical ways to use it that you can copy directly.



### 1. Let AI Track Hot Topics for You

I sent one line to OpenClaw on QQ: help me get trending AI-related news.

After a while, it replied with a neat summary of the current hot topics:

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/ce2501ffae6c213e1b07f570ac32464b.jpg)

In the past, I might have had to scroll through news sites myself. Now one message is enough~

If needed, you can also let it run a scheduled task. For example: “Every morning at 8, check whether there are any new OpenClaw community tricks.” Then it will quietly keep an eye on it and push updates to you the moment something appears.



### 2. Idea Recorder

Sometimes when I’m walking around, I suddenly get a good idea or remember something important, so I open my phone’s notes app to write it down before I forget.

Over time, the number of saved notes grows, and many of the things I wrote down end up being ignored anyway.

Now I can simply use OpenClaw as my super notepad. First, I assign it a role by telling it something like, “You are an idea recorder”:

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209180148160.png)

After that, whenever I have an idea or something I’m afraid I’ll forget, I can just pull out my phone and send one sentence to AI in QQ.

Unlike a normal notes app, OpenClaw doesn’t just record things—it also fixes typos, organizes categories, and never forgets anything!

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209180446618.png)



### 3. A Super Programmer You Carry with You

This is probably the most exciting scenario for programmer friends.

Because OpenClaw runs on a server, it can directly write code, run programs, and deploy services on that server. Once it’s done, you can use the result immediately.

For example, while I’m riding the subway, I can pull out my phone and send one sentence to OpenClaw on QQ:

> 帮我写一个网页小工具，能上传图片后批量压缩，支持调整压缩质量，写完直接部署到服务器上让我能访问。

A few minutes later, AI replies: the tool is done, it’s already deployed, and you can use it directly by visiting URL XX.

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209183510372.png)

That’s right—just one message, and a usable online tool appears like magic.

> Note: if it’s inaccessible, the corresponding port on the server firewall may not have been opened.

![](https://pic.yupi.icu/1/image-20260209183750274.png)

From then on, whenever inspiration strikes, you don’t even need to pull out your computer. You can create runnable projects purely by chatting with AI on your phone.

That feeling… how should I put it? It’s got a bit of that Iron Man vibe.
